Privacy Policy

Clockwork Cosmos, by Wait For It Software
Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Clockwork Cosmos (the "game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Wait For It Software ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at edwin.r.lau@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account, and we do not run servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one. Those ads come from Google AdMob, which collects device information as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels reached, stars, best times), your settings (volume, vibration, left-handed layout, reduced motion), your windings, which hints you have unlocked and which hint packs you own. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us. You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, and uninstalling the game removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to unlock a hint solution or refill a winding. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When the game loads or shows an ad, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was viewed or completed).

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies: see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some U.S. states), the game asks for your consent or offers you the choice to opt out before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy when that option is shown. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings under Google → Ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

Hint packs are sold through Google Play's billing system. Google processes your payment and handles your payment information under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock the hints and restore them if you reinstall. We do not receive your name, card number or billing address.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information directly, and we do not sell personal information. The game does not include analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

Children

Clockwork Cosmos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help address it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states, or the EEA and UK), you may have rights to know about, access, delete or opt out of certain uses of personal information, including "selling" or "sharing" it for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold personal information about you ourselves, the most direct ways to use these rights are:

  • change your ad consent or opt-out choice in Settings → Ad Privacy, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings;
  • delete your game data with Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling the game;
  • contact Google about data it holds, through your Google Account.

You can also contact us at edwin.r.lau@gmail.com, and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
